The single most important decision was to make every write into the FHIR service idempotent and every replay approval-gated: each resource gets a deterministic ID derived from the partner's business identifier, a partnerId:batchId ledger is checked before any retry, and every replay needs a recorded approval within that partner's limit (10,000 messages for Northwind, 5,000 resources for Fabrikam, full batch for Litware). INC-2026-052 showed that retries which cannot tell a lost write from a failed one create duplicate clinical resources. With only four engineers on call, the automation has to be safe to re-run without anyone checking every record by hand.
