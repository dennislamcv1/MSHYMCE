I kept the synchronous scoring path to a single Azure ML managed online endpoint backed by a clustered Redis feature store, and moved telemetry, drift detection, retraining triggers and audit capture onto an asynchronous Event Hubs and Stream Analytics stream, so drift is alerted within two hours without adding latency to the 120 ms budget. Every release passes a pre-traffic load test, a 20% mirrored shadow and a 10% canary on the same endpoint, and the previous model stays warm at 0% traffic, which turns rollback into a traffic switch that automation completes in under five minutes. Gates, thresholds and retention rules live in version-controlled configuration read by both the pipeline and the monitors, so automation does the time-critical work and the two-person team makes the judgment calls.
